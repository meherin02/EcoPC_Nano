Decision guide

ACTIVE · Recent keyboard or mouse input.

BACKGROUND WORK · Input inactive, CPU above the configured threshold.

IDLE WITH MOTION · Input inactive and CPU low, but PIR sees movement. Warning withheld.

TEMPORARILY AWAY · Waiting for continuous no-motion confirmation.

SUSPECTED WASTE · Input inactive, CPU low, and no motion beyond the grace period. Reading or video playback may still be ongoing.

KEEP AWAKE · The user paused EcoPC warnings.

UNKNOWN · Required data unavailable; waste warning withheld.

Motion alone does not mark the PC active. PIR can miss people sitting still.

Audible warning: a short beep from this PC’s speakers every 5 seconds while suspected waste is active.
